Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our expense-tracking service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic usage information, such as login timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate your account, display and summarize your expense data, and maintain the security of the service. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal or financial data with third parties, except where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Spendly and its users.

5. Your Choices

You can review, edit, or delete the expense data in your account at any time. You may also request deletion of your account and associated data.

6. Cookies

Spendly may use minimal session cookies to keep you signed in. We do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking.

7. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for use by children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to our support team through the contact details listed on our website.
